The 2B: when a breakdown fails

The second low dips under the first, shakes out nervous sellers, then reverses. Traders call it a 2B.

  1. 1Price falls from 220 and finds a first bottom at 200.
  2. 2It bounces to 210. That high becomes the trigger line.
  3. 3It breaks below 200 to 195. Sellers panic, but the move fails.
  4. 4A close above 210 is the buy. The stop-loss sits under the trap low, at 195.
  5. 5Drag the stop-loss or target and see how risk and reward change.

Find the floor and the ceiling

Support is where buyers keep stepping in. Resistance is where sellers keep taking profit. The more often price turns at a level, the more it matters.

  1. 1Drag each line until it touches three turning points.
  2. 2Price closes above resistance: a breakout.
  3. 3It pulls back to the old ceiling, which now holds as a floor. That retest is the safer entry.
  4. 4The trend continues. Resistance near 1,548 is now support.
