# Product

**SMISHA Share Market Classes** — stock-market training classes in Kharadi, Pune (trainer: Rahul Kotkar). Tagline: "Learn. Analyse. Grow."

## This product
An interactive, slide-by-slide website version of SMISHA's seminar deck. Each slide is a full-screen scene; anything with prices or numbers is drawn live (candles, tables, calculators) instead of shown as a picture.

## Audience and use
- Primary: a live seminar audience watching it projected on a 16:9 screen while the trainer advances with a clicker or arrow keys.
- Secondary: prospective students opening the shared link on a phone.
- Most viewers are beginners to the market; many have lost money trading before.

## What the deck must achieve
Make the market feel learnable, show SMISHA's method (price action, patterns, risk management, psychology), and lead to registering for the course.

## Brand commitments
- Logo: gold bull over rising bars, "SMISHA / SHARE MARKET CLASSES / Learn. Analyse. Grow." Keep as supplied.
- Colours: deep navy ground, logo gold, green up-candles, red down-candles.
- Tone: confident, practical, plain English. No get-rich-quick hype.

## Constraints
- Works offline at a seminar (self-hosted fonts and libraries).
- Every scene readable from the back of a room: large type, no hover-only features.
- Footer disclaimer: "For learning only. Investments in securities markets are subject to market risks."
- Illustrative price data is labelled as illustrative. No invented performance claims.
- Content source: docs/slide-content.md.
